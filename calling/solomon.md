# Solomon: the Wise King

Young Solomon inherits the throne of Israel and faces the overwhelming task of leading God's chosen people. His journey from a humble child to the wisest king reveals how the LORD empowers those who trust in Him rather than their own strength.

## The Problem of an Inadequate Leader

Solomon was the son of David, born into a royal household after a period of intense conflict (2 Samuel 12:24). The political environment was fragile as David's older sons had already attempted to seize the throne (1 Kings 1:5-10). The nation of Israel was a great people, too many to be numbered or counted for multitude (1 Kings 3:8). There was no permanent house for the name of the LORD, and the people were still sacrificing at high places (1 Kings 3:2). Solomon himself was but a little child who did not know how to go out or come in (1 Kings 3:7). He was tasked with governing a people he felt inadequate to lead (1 Kings 3:9). The problem was that Solomon lacked the wisdom and experience necessary to govern the vast and complex kingdom of Israel effectively (1 Kings 3:7-9).

## Solomon's Humble Condition

Solomon loved the LORD and walked in the statutes of David his father (1 Kings 3:3). He went to Gibeon, the great high place, to offer sacrifices (1 Kings 3:4). He offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar (1 Kings 3:4). This massive act of worship and sacrifice preceded God's response (1 Kings 3:4-5). It was at Gibeon that the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream by night (1 Kings 3:5).

## The Divine Calling

The LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream and offered to give him whatever he asked. Later, after the temple was built, God appeared again to establish His covenant and promise His presence.

> At Gibeon the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream by night, and God said, “**Ask what I shall give you**.”
>
> — 1 Kings 3:5 (ESV)

> And the LORD said to him, “I have heard your prayer and your plea, which you have made before Me. **I have consecrated this house** that you have built, by putting My name there forever. **My eyes and My heart will be there for all time**. And as for you, if you will walk before Me, as David your father walked, with integrity of heart and uprightness, doing according to all that I have commanded you, and keeping My statutes and My rules, then **I will establish your royal throne over Israel forever**, as I promised David your father, saying, ‘You shall not lack a man on the throne of Israel.’”
>
> — 1 Kings 9:3-5 (ESV)

## The Necessary Sacrifice

Solomon had to sacrifice his own pride and desire for personal gain (1 Kings 3:11). He could have asked for long life, riches, or the life of his enemies (1 Kings 3:11). Instead, he risked being seen as weak by admitting his limitations as a "little child" (1 Kings 3:7). He sacrificed the opportunity to pursue selfish ambitions to serve the LORD'S people (1 Kings 3:9).

## Solomon's Initial Response

Solomon responded with deep humility and gratitude (1 Kings 3:6). He acknowledged the steadfast love God had shown to his father David (1 Kings 3:6). He did not rebel or refuse the calling, but rather admitted his own inability to fulfill it alone (1 Kings 3:7). He focused on his responsibility to the people God had chosen (1 Kings 3:8).

## Solomon's Eventual Reaction

Solomon asked the LORD for an understanding mind to govern the people and to discern between good and evil (1 Kings 3:9). This request pleased the LORD (1 Kings 3:10). After the dream, Solomon went to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the covenant of the LORD (1 Kings 3:15). He offered up burnt offerings and peace offerings and made a feast for all his servants (1 Kings 3:15). He demonstrated his trust by immediately acting on the wisdom he received (1 Kings 3:16-28). When two women brought a difficult dispute over a child, Solomon used his God-given discernment to reveal the true mother (1 Kings 3:27). He also dedicated himself to the massive project of building the house of the LORD, which took seven years (1 Kings 6:38). He successfully finished the temple and his own house, accomplishing all he had planned (2 Chronicles 7:11). Solomon continued to offer sacrifices three times a year, maintaining his devotion (1 Kings 9:25).

## The Divine Miracle

God granted Solomon unparalleled wisdom and added riches and honor that he did not even ask for. He also confirmed His presence with fire from heaven.

> “Behold, **I now do according to your word. Behold, I give you a wise and discerning mind**, so that none like you has been before you and none like you shall arise after you. **I give you also what you have not asked, both riches and honor**, so that no other king shall compare with you, all your days.”
>
> — 1 Kings 3:12-13 (ESV)

> As soon as Solomon finished his prayer, **fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of the LORD filled the temple**.
>
> — 2 Chronicles 7:1 (ESV)

## The Global Result

The miracle of Solomon's wisdom resulted in justice and awe throughout all Israel (1 Kings 3:28). The people perceived that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice (1 Kings 3:28). His fame spread to all the surrounding nations, and kings from across the earth sought his counsel (1 Kings 4:29-34, 10:24). The nation enjoyed a period of peace and unprecedented prosperity (1 Kings 4:20-25). The Queen of Sheba visited and was breathless at the wisdom and wealth God had bestowed upon him (1 Kings 10:4-5). Most importantly, the presence of the LORD dwelt in the temple, providing a place for the people to worship and seek forgiveness (2 Chronicles 7:14-16).

## Conclusion

Solomon faced the problem of leading a vast nation while feeling like an inadequate child. He was limited by his youth and lack of experience, but he responded to God with humility and a request for wisdom. The LORD promised him a discerning mind and far exceeded his expectations by adding riches and honor. God intervened by filling the temple with His glory and providing the wisdom needed to govern justly. The result was a golden age of peace and prosperity for Israel that influenced the entire world. Solomon sacrificed his selfish desires for the sake of his people, and in the end, he gained a legacy as the wisest king to ever live. His story proves that when we seek God's kingdom first, He is faithful to provide everything we need and more.
