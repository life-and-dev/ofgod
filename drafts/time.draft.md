# Reference

There was a time before our time.

A time where everything was void.

Time is the 4^th^ dimension which allow 3D objects to move, interact or
create events.

Time was created. God exist outside time.

Time is a resource that gets spended.

# People relate to time in different ways

All three designs are God's design.

## Linear Time

Time flows sequentially. You order your day sequentially. Every day is
the same. Your productivity is the same any time of the day. You thrive
in the 8:00 -- 17:00 day.

Analytical, task focused people.

Good at checking things off the list.

Most western world organizations work this way and is forced onto the
people.

Usually: Left brain

**Challenge**: Find it hard to be creative. Happy to be in a set pattern
or structure.

## Cyclical Time

Time flow in circles. There are certain times that are more productive.
People are more productive in the morning than later in the day or the
other way around.

Can even track seasons. Some weeks are more productive than other weeks.

Can still get things done, but are sometimes more productive than other
times.

Could be: Left or right brain

**Challenge**: To be still productive in times they are down. To arrange
the most challenging/intensive tasks in "up" time.

## Pulse Time

Up time = they are on fire, they can forget to eat, take shower etc.
Very high burst of productivity. Can deliver fantastic projects in
record time.

Down time = you cannot get anything out of them. Everyone has to wait
for them.

Usually find it hard to keep a steady job. Boss expects 8:00-17:00 but
these people might experience a "down time" and when the office wants to
close at 17:00 then these people might be on fire and wants to work late
to finish the project.

There people either jumps from job to job often or they find a boss that
loves their creativity so much that he is willing to be put up with all
their "down time" or they do something complete independent
(entrepreneurs).

Usually: Right brain

**Challenge:** To discipline yourself to still function in your "down
time". Use the "down time" to line up tasks so that when your next
"pulse" comes, you know what to do and won't waste time. Might need to
negotiate times with employer for best performance as an employee.

# The beginning marker of time

Each person's life has personal timeline that fits into the flow of
human history.

Each person has a "spiritual marker" that indicates where your timeline
starts in the context of the world's timeline.

Your "spiritual market" could be "off" that causes:

- hard to do deliverance

- re-experience pain from the past

- time is not in sync (missing opportunities)

- always feel uncomfortable about time


Causes of incorrect "spiritual marker":

- no celebration of your conception or birth (e.g. experience grief
  like when the mother died while delivering the baby)

- scheduled birth (get born on the wrong day)


Incorrect "spiritual market" could be fixed. Ask God to move the
"spiritual market" to the right place. It might require repentance,
cleansing.

Ask God to align your time (rest of it) with His timeline. People who
has done this said they are experiencing peace in their spirit.

# Alignment

When you wish that you were still living in the past. "I wish I were
still living in the good old days." You are saying, you do not want to
be in the present. That gives the enemy the right to move you back into
the past at a point that he chooses -- a bad time -- so that you
re-experience bad things that you already have dealt with/delivered.

Repent that you rejected the present time. Ask God to fix the time
again. E.g. "Please bring spirit, body and soul back into alignment with
God's time."

If you go through a difficult time, do not live in denial. Realize it is
a difficult time but see the pain as "productive pain" and grow as much
as you can during that period.

# Defiled Time

Time is like a battery that absorb.

Personal timeline can become defiled. Could be a particular day, a
specific month, etc.

Time is actually cyclical. E.g. Christmas happens every year. Some
experience it as a good time to be excited. Some has a defiled Christmas
and every year there is even more pain and defilement.

Defiled time could happen on a personal level or even on a national
level.

What defiles time?

Sin: could be your own or an ancestor

Repent, ask for compensation in whatever way God wants to.
