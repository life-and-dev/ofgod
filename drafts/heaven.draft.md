---
description: "Heaven — Path: creation / heaven"
keywords: "Heaven, creation"
---

- It was created by God (Genesis 1:1, 2:1, 2 King 19:15; Psalm 124:8; Psalm
  134:3; Prov 3:19; Prov 8:27; Acts 4:24)

  - God created the things in Heaven (Colossians1:16)
- It is a place somewhere high above us (Genesis 6:17, Genesis 49:25, Josh
  2:11, Psalm 14:2; Psalm 113:4; Psalm 139:8; Isaiah 63:15, Ezekiel 8:3; Amos 9:2; Luke
  24:51; John 1:32; Rev 4:1, Rev 5:3, Rev 11:12)

- It is owned by God (Genesis 14:19-22, 1 Chr 29:11, 2 Chr 2:12)

- Some disasters that comes from "heaven" is caused by Satan (Job 1)

- It exists in the spiritual realm (Rev 4:2)

- There is a throne set in heaven: God + 24 elders (2 Chr 18:18; Psalm
  11:4; Isaiah 66:1; Mark 16:19; Rev 4)

- There are different creatures in heaven who can praise God (Rev 4,
  Rev 5:13)

- There is sound in heaven (Rev 8:1, Rev 11:15, Rev 11:19, Rev 12:10,
  Rev 18:4, Rev 19:1)

- God's temple and ark is in heaven (Rev 11:19, Rev 15:5)

- A war can break out in heaven (Rev 12:7, Rev 19:14)

- There are angels in heaven (Rev 18:1, Rev 20:1)

- There are great multitudes in heaven (Rev 19:1)

- There will be a new heaven and a new earth (Isaiah 65:17, 66:22; Mark
  13:31; 2 Peter 3:10-13; Rev 21:1)

  - It won't have a sea (Rev 21:1)

  - There will be a new holy city (Hebrews 11:16; Rev 21:2)

    - Believers are citizens of Heaven (Philippians 3:20; Hebrews 11:16)

    - Believers in Heaven will receive a transformed body (Philippians
      3:21)

    - Believers receives an inheritance in heaven (1 Peter 1:4)

  - The tabernacle of God will then be with men (Rev 21:3)

    - It won't have a temple (Rev 21:22)
  - There won't be: death, sorrow, pain (Rev 21:4)

  - All things will be made new (Rev 21:5)

  - The water of life will be available for free (Rev 21:6)

  - There will be no sun or moon because God's glory will illuminate
    it (Rev 21:22)

  - It will be a pure place (Rev 21:27)
