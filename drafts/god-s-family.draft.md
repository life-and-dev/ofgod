The bible tells us that we are not the result of some random evolution:

> **[God](/god) [created](/creation) [man](/creation/human) in His own image**, in the image of God He created him; [male and female](/creation/human) He created them. -- Genesis 1:27 (ESV)

Why?

> To the [faithful](/life/faith) in [Christ](/god/son/christ) Jesus... Blessed be the God and [Father](/god/father) of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places, even as He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world, that 
> 
> 1. **we should be holy and blameless before Him**. 
> 2. **In love** He predestined us **for adoption to Himself as sons** through Jesus Christ, according to **the purpose of His will**, 
> 3. to the **praise** of His glorious grace, with which He has blessed us in the Beloved. 
>
> -- Ephesians 1:1,3-6 (ESV)

God could have made robots who were programmed to infinitely praise Him or sing hymns, but what honour is in that? Instead, He created us in his own image to have a free will of choice so that we can decide (Josua 24:15; Proverbs 3:31), for example:
* to trust God (Psalm 37:5-6) or something else 
* to obey God (Matthew 7:18-27; John 3:33-36) or rebel against Him 
* to praise God (Psalm 145:3; Psalm 150:6; Hebrews 13:15) or to blaspheme Him 
* to worship and serve God (Psalm 29:2; Luke 4:8; John 4:24) or an idol  
* to love God (Hosea 6:6) or reject Him 

When we choose to be "faithful in Christ Jesus" we honour God. God won't force anyone to be His child.

## Our Father's Spirit

The Father's Spirit allows us to access Him while we are on Earth.

> For through him *(Christ Jesus)* we both have **access in one Spirit to the Father**. So then you are no longer strangers and aliens, but **you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God**, built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, [Christ](/god/son/christ) Jesus himself being the cornerstone, in whom the whole structure, being joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord. In him you also are being built together into **a dwelling place for God** by the Spirit. -- Ephesians 2:14-22 (ESV)

> ***“Here are my mother and my brothers! For whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my brother and sister and mother.”***

Another example:

> And the King will answer them, Truly, I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these **my brothers**, you did it to me.’ -- Matthew 25:40 (ESV)

Paul taught:

> For those whom he foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, in order that he might be **the firstborn among many brothers**. -- Romans 8:29 (ESV)

# Our Human Families

> Therefore **be imitators of God, as beloved children**. -- Ephesians 5:1 (ESV)

This is good news for those who lost their earthly families:

> Jesus said, “Truly, I say to you, there is no one who has left house or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or lands, for my sake and for the gospel, who will not receive a hundredfold now **in this time**, houses and brothers and sisters and mothers and children and lands, with persecutions, and in the age to come eternal life. -- Mark 10:29-30 (ESV)

Just as the Father loved Jesus, and Jesus has loved us, we ought to love our adopted human family members also:

> “**As the Father loved Me, I also have loved you**; abide in my love. 
> 
> If you keep My commandments, you will abide in my love, just as I have kept my Father’s commandments and abide in His love. These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may remain in you, and that your joy may be full. 
> 
> This is my commandment, that **you love one another as I have loved you**. 
> 
> -- John 15:9-12 (NKJV)
