Body = Flesh (Gen1; Ecclesiastes 11:1)

- Beauty is temporary and eventually dies (Isaiah 40:6-8)
- The body dies when the Spirit is removed (James 2:26)
- Suitable/Pleasant/Graceful words brings health to the bones (Prov 16:24)
- Flesh has a will of its own (Romans 8:1-16)
    - The will of the flesh is passions / lust: adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lustfulness, idolatry, sorcery, hatreds, fighting, jealousies, anger, rivalries, divisions, heresies, envying, murders, drunkennesses, reveling (Galatians 5:16-25; 1 Corinthians 3:1-5)
    - Will of the flesh reap corruption (Galatians 6:8) or death (Romans 8:1-16)
    - Will of the Holy Spirit reaps everlasting life (Galatians 6:8; Romans 8:1-16)
- The body should be presented as a living sacrifice: holy and acceptable to God (Romans 12:1)
