> “I watched till thrones were put in place,<br/>
> And **the Ancient of Days was seated**;<br/>
> His garment was white as snow, and the hair of His head was like pure wool.<br/>
> His throne was a fiery flame,<br/>
> Its wheels a burning fire;<br/>
> A fiery stream issued and came forth from before Him.
>
> A thousand thousands ministered to Him;<br/>
> Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before Him.
> 
> The court was seated, and the books were opened.
> 
> -- Daniel 7:10 (NKJV)

The "Ancient of Days" is God. Notice that "[the Son of Man](/god/son/essence/of-man/human)" is a separate person:

> “I was watching in the night visions, and behold, One like the Son of Man,<br/>
> Coming with the clouds of heaven! (Revelation 1:7)<br/>
> He **came to** the Ancient of Days,<br/>
> And they **brought him** near before Him.
>
> Then to him was given dominion and glory and a kingdom, (Matthew 24:30)<br/>
> That all peoples, nations, and languages should serve him.<br/>
> His dominion is an everlasting dominion,<br/>
> Which shall not pass away, and his kingdom the one which shall not be destroyed.
>
> -- Daniel 7:14

Then the interpretation follows:

> Then the kingdom and dominion, and the greatness of the kingdoms under the whole heaven, shall be given to the people, the saints of the Most High.
> 
> His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey him.
>
> -- Daniel 7:27 (NKJV)

However, note that:

* the "one like **the Son of Man**" means Daniel saw a human
* the "one like the son of man" **came to** "the Ancient of Days" which means he saw 2 characters, a human and "the Ancient of Days". Jesus is most likely "the Son of Man" (Matthew 8:20; 9:6; 10:23; 11:19; 12:8, 40; 13:41; 16:13,27,28; 17:9,12,22; 18:11; 19:28; 20:18; 20:28; 24:27,30,37,29,44; 25:13,31; 26:2,2445,64; Mark 2:10,28; 8:31,38; 9:9,12,31; 10:33,45; 13:26; 14:21,41,62; Luke 5:24; 6:5; 7:34; 9:22,26,44,56,58; 11:30; 12:8,10,40; 17:22,24,30; 18:8,31; 19:10; 21:27,36; 22:22,48,69; 24:7; John 1:51; 3:13,14; 5:27; 6:27,53,62; 8:28; 12:23,34; 13:31; Acts 7:56; Revelation 1:13; 14:14)
* the human person was **given** dominion and glory, by "the Ancient of Days". This implies that his authority was given by his God.
