# Trusting God

There are more than 100 verses in the Old Testament about **trusting** God but very few about *faith*. However, in the New Testament it is the other way around. More than 200 verses highlight the importance of **faith**, but it says relatively little about trusting God.

The reason is that, unlike the Hebrew Old Testament, the Greek New Testament word *pistis* was translated as [faith](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/faith), which means **trust**. That is why the prophets, Jesus, and the apostles bore witness to who God is.

## Importance of Trusting God

> Thus says the LORD:
>
> | Cursed is the man who trusts in man                                                            | Blessed is the man who trusts in the LORD                                                                                       |
> | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
> | ... and makes flesh his strength, whose heart departs from the LORD.                           | ... and whose hope is the LORD.                                                                                                 |
> | For he shall be like a shrub in the desert, and shall not see when good comes,                 | For he shall be like a tree planted by the waters, which spreads out its roots by the river, and will not fear when heat comes; |
> | but shall inhabit the parched places in the wilderness, in a salt land which is not inhabited. | but its leaf will be green, and will not be anxious in the year of drought, nor will cease from yielding fruit.                 |
>
> — Jeremiah 17:5-8 (NKJV)

Likewise,

> His delight is not in the strength of the horse, nor his pleasure in the legs of a man, but **the LORD takes pleasure in those who fear Him**, in **those who hope in His steadfast love**. — Psalm 147:11 (ESV)

And again,

> Without *faith* it is **impossible to please Him** *[God]*... — Hebrews 11:6 (ESV)

This pattern appears throughout Scripture: God repeatedly calls His people to trust Him.

From the beginning, in the Garden of Eden, God required Adam and Eve to trust and rely on His wisdom rather than seek wisdom on their own (Genesis 3).

Without trusting God, people lack God's provision, protection, and authority. To deal with this lack, people [commit sin as a coping mechanism](https://kingdom.ofgod.info/darkness#the-battle-for-control).

## The fear of the Lord

> Come, you children, listen to me; I will teach you the ***fear*** of the LORD:
>
> Who is the man who desires life, and loves many days, that he may see good?
>
> * **Keep your tongue from evil, and your lips from speaking deceit**.
> * **Depart from evil and do good**;
> * **Seek peace and pursue it**.
>
> — Psalm 34:11-14 (NKJV)

Without "fear" we resort to sin.

According to Strong's Concordance, the Hebrew word translated as "fear" could also mean to be in awe of God (reverence).

|                   | [Strong's Concordance](https://biblehub.com/hebrew/3374.htm) |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Original Word     | יִרְאָה                                                         |
| Part of Speech    | Noun Feminine                                                |
| Transliteration   | yir'ah                                                       |
| Pronunciation     | yee-RAH                                                      |
| Phonetic Spelling | yir-aw'                                                      |
| KJV               | X dreadful, X exceedingly, fear(-fulness)                    |
| NASB              | fear, reverence, awesome, fearing                            |

The more awesome God is to us, the smaller our problems look, and the easier it is to trust God.

> You keep him in perfect peace **whose mind is stayed on You**, because he trusts in You. Trust in the LORD forever, for the LORD GOD is an everlasting rock. — Isaiah 26:3-4 (ESV)

The sons of Korah also wrote that their God is greater than the greatest problems they face:

> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
>
> **Therefore** we will not fear though the earth give way, though the mountains be moved into the heart of the sea, though its waters roar and foam, though the mountains tremble at its swelling. Selah
>
> — Psalm 46:1-3 (ESV)

Paul also reminded his congregation that God's glory is greater than their sufferings:

> For I consider that the sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the [glory](https://word.ofgod.info/terms/glory) that is to be revealed to us.
>
> ...
>
> What then shall we say to these things?  
> If God is for us, who can be against us?
>
> — Romans 8:18,31 (ESV)

## Conclusion

Biblical [trusting God](#trusting-god) is not mere religious language. It is a life of depending on Him rather than on human strength, as shown in [Importance of Trusting God](#importance-of-trusting-god).

This trust is strengthened by [The fear of the Lord](#the-fear-of-the-lord), where reverence for God turns the heart from evil, steadies it in trouble, and teaches it to hope in Him.
