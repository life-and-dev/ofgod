> There are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit. There are differences of ministries, but the same Lord. -- 1 Corinthians 12:4-5 (NKJV)

# Office Gifts

> He Himself gave 
> 
> 1. some to be apostles, 
> 2. some prophets, 
> 3. some evangelists, 
> 4. and some pastors and
> 5. teachers, 
> 
> for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ
> 
> -- Ephesians 4:11-12 (NKJV)

As the above scripture states, "Offices" are given by Jesus for the equipping of the saints.

# [Manifestation Gifts](manifestation)

> 1. For to one is given the word of wisdom through the Spirit, 
> 2. to another the word of knowledge through the same Spirit, 
> 3. to another faith by the same Spirit, 
> 4. to another gifts of healings by the same Spirit, 
> 5. to another the working of miracles, 
> 6. to another [prophecy](manifestation/prophecy), 
> 7. to another discerning of spirits, 
> 8. to another different kinds of tongues, 
> 9. to another the interpretation of tongues. 
>
> -- 1 Corinthians 12:8-10 (NKJV)

[Manifestation Gifts](manifestation) are given by the Holy Spirit for edification. These are gifts imparted and can be sought by any believer.

# [Redemptive Gifts](/life/design/redemptive)

> Having gifts that differ according to the grace given to us, let us use them:
> 
> 1. if [prophecy](/life/design/redemptive/prophet), in proportion to our faith; 
> 2. if [service](/life/design/redemptive/servant), in our serving; 
> 3. [the one who teaches](/life/design/redemptive/teacher), in his teaching; 
> 4. [the one who exhorts](/life/design/redemptive/exhorter), in his exhortation; 
> 5. [the one who contributes](/life/design/redemptive/giver), in generosity; 
> 6. [the one who leads](/life/design/redemptive/ruler), with zeal; 
> 7. [the one who does acts of mercy](/life/design/redemptive/mercy), with cheerfulness.
>
> -- Romans 12:6-8 (ESV)

Your [Redemptive Gift](/life/design/redemptive) is part of your core design. It is received at conception and defines the soul, spirit, personality, outlook and how you operate. Like a "spiritual DNA".
