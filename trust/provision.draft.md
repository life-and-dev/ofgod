# Provision

Of David, when he changed his behavior before Abimelech, so that he drove him out, and he went away (1 Samuel 21:10-15):

> Oh, **[fear the LORD](#the-fear-of-the-lord)**, you His [saints](https://biblehub.com/hebrew/6918.htm)!
>
> There is no want to **[those who fear Him](#the-fear-of-the-lord)**.
>
> The young lions lack and suffer hunger; But **those who *[seek (ask/cares about/inquire)](https://biblehub.com/hebrew/1875.htm)* the LORD** shall not lack any good thing.
>
> -- Psalm 34:9-10 (NKJV)

This is what Jesus taught during his first recorded sermon by Matthew:

> “Therefore I say to you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink; nor about your body, what you will put on.
>
> Is not life more than food and the body more than clothing?
>
> Look at the birds of the air, for they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns; yet **your [heavenly Father](/god/father) feeds them**.
>
> **Are you not of more value** than they?
> Which of you by worrying can add one cubit to his stature?
> So why do you worry about clothing?
>
> Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin; and yet I say to you that even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these. Now if **God so clothes the grass** of the field, which today is, and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, **will He not much more clothe you**, O you of little [faith](/life/faith)?
>
> Therefore do not worry, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’ For after all these things the Gentiles seek. For your heavenly Father knows that you need all these things.
>
> But seek first [the kingdom of God](/kingdom) and [His righteousness](/bible/concepts/righteousness), and all these things shall be added to you.
>
> -- Matthew 6:25-33 (NKJV)

King David shared this advice during his senior years (Psalm 37:25):

> **Do not fret** because of evildoers, **nor be envious** of the workers of iniquity. For they shall soon be cut down like the grass, and wither as the green herb.
>
> **Trust in the LORD**, and **do good**; **Dwell in the land**, and **feed on His faithfulness**.
>
> **Delight yourself also in the LORD**, and He shall give you the desires of your heart.
>
> **Commit your way to the LORD**, **trust also in Him**, and He shall bring it to pass.
>
> -- Psalm 37:1-5 (NKJV)

King David also gave this simple confession of trust:

> **The LORD is my shepherd**; **I shall not want**.
>
> -- Psalm 23:1 (NKJV)

The sons of Korah also described the LORD's generosity this way:

> For the LORD God is a sun and shield;
>
> The LORD will give grace and glory;
>
> **No good thing will He withhold** from those who walk uprightly.
>
> -- Psalm 84:11 (NKJV)

Paul grounded confidence in God's provision in the gift of His Son:

> He who did not spare His own Son, but delivered Him up for us all, how shall He not with Him also **freely give us all things**?
>
> -- Romans 8:32 (NKJV)

He also wrote that God is able to supply believers for every good work:

> And **God is able to make all grace abound toward you**, that you, always having **all sufficiency in all things**, may have an abundance for every good work.
>
> -- 2 Corinthians 9:8 (NKJV)

And he gave this direct promise about need:

> And my God **shall supply all your need** according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus.
>
> -- Philippians 4:19 (NKJV)

Hebrews adds this encouragement to live free from fear about material need:

> Let your conduct be without covetousness; be content with such things as you have. For He Himself has said, **“I will never leave you nor forsake you.”**
>
> So we may boldly say:
>
> **“The LORD is my helper;**
>
> **I will not fear.**
>
> **What can man do to me?”**
>
> -- Hebrews 13:5-6 (NKJV)

## Conclusion

Believers do not need to live in anxious striving. The Father's care is sure. Those who seek His kingdom, fear the LORD, walk uprightly, and learn contentment can trust Him to provide what is good and needed. Because He is with His people, they can refuse worry and live without fear.
