## Oppression

Paul also encouraged the oppressed (Romans 8:18-23) with the words:

> We know that all things work together for good to those who love God, to those who are the [called according to His purpose](/life/purpose). -- Romans 8:28 NKJV

## Peace

> Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything **by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God**. And **the peace of God**, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus. -- Philippians 4:6-7 (ESV)

## Conclusion

God protects those who fear Him, trust Him, dwell in Him, and follow Christ. He is their refuge, their keeper, their guard against the evil one, and their eternal security in the hands of the Son and the Father.

This does not mean believers never suffer, for the righteous have many afflictions. It means God delivers, preserves, and keeps His people through trouble, and gives peace even in the middle of it.
